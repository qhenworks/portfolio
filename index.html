Terms of Service

Ownership

Please DO NOT claim my work as your own. Proper credit must be given when the work is published under the name qhen (@_qhen).

If the work wants to be uncredited, there will be an additional fee.

Commercial Fee

If the project is being used for commercial purposes, including monetized music videos, original songs, promotional content, or anything else that generates revenue, there will be an additional commercial fee of 100% of the original project price.

Right to Decline Commissions

I have the right to decline any commission, especially if the project is outside of what I can do, exceeds my current capabilities, or if the terms do not work with my workflow and working conditions.

Revisions

Minor revisions are unlimited when it is fixing mistakes from my side, such as typos or small errors.

If the revision changes from the original concept or scope that was agreed on, an additional revision fee will be charged.

Refunds

Refunds and cancellations are not available once I have started working on the project.

If the project gets cancelled after I have already started working, any payment that has already been made will not be refunded and will be forfeited.

File Retention

If a project stays inactive because the client stops communicating for more than six (6) months, I cannot guarantee that the project files, assets, backups, or working files will still be available.

If the project starts again after this period and the files are no longer available, recreating assets or rebuilding parts of the project may require additional fees and changes to the timeline.

Commission Guideline

Assets and Material

All assets and materials MUST be prepared and sent before production starts. If there's sudden changes of assets or add-ons, addtional fees may be charged (eg. music, illustrations, logos, or any other resources needed for the project).

Resolution and Framerate

All projects are made in 1920 x 1080 at 24 FPS by default.

Higher resolution exports up to 4K need to be requested at the beginning of the project, addtional fee may be charged.

Down Payment

A down payment of at least 50% of the total project price is required before I can start the project.

References and Ideas

If you have specific references or ideas for the project, you MUST provide CLEAR GUIDELINES or a STORYBOARD before production starts.

If there are no clear references or guidelines, the creative direction and visual interpretation of the project will be left to my artistic discretion.
